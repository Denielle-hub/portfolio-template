Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

What should I do if I cannot sign in?

Check your internet connection, confirm your login details, and use the official password recovery process if needed.

What information helps with troubleshooting?

Describe what happened, when it started, the steps you already tried, and any error message. Never share your password.

What if a payment appears delayed?

Review the transaction status and relevant processing details. If the issue continues, contact the provider through its official support channel.

How do I report a suspicious account request?

Do not share verification codes or sensitive details. Use the service's official security or support channel to report it.
