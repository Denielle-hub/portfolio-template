A Clear Support Process

From problem to next step.

A structured intake experience helps customers explain their concerns without needing to know technical terminology.

01 / IDENTIFY

Describe the symptom

Capture what the user was trying to do, what happened instead, and any relevant error message.

02 / TROUBLESHOOT

Record what was tried

Document basic checks already completed so the next support step can be more focused.

03 / FOLLOW UP

Clarify the next action

Explain the next step in plain language and identify when further technical review may be needed.

Before You Submit

Helpful details make troubleshooting easier.

Include the affected feature, the approximate time the issue occurred, the steps leading up to it, and any non-sensitive error message. Never share your password, one-time code, or full payment details in a support request.
